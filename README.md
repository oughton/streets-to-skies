# From Streets to Skies

A single-player browser game. You start out homeless and work your way up to airline pilot.

**Play it:** https://oughton.github.io/streets-to-skies/

## The jobs

1. **Busker.** Play the arrow sequence in each passer-by's speech bubble before they walk off. Collect **£8**.
2. **Baker.** Take flour, knead it (tap ← → ← →), bake it, then sell it at the counter. Take the bread out while the oven bar is in the green zone. Earn **£80**.
3. **Mechanic.** Change tyres (press the arrow on each wheel nut, then ↑ to swap the tyre) and paint cars (steer the spray gun over the whole body). Reach **£1,000**.
4. **Pilot.** Speed up along the runway, take off, and dodge birds and storms. Then land gently on the destination runway. Complete **5 flights** to win.

## Controls

The whole game is played with the **arrow keys**. **M** turns the sound on and off. Progress is saved in your browser.

## Running locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. There is no build step.
